01  /  Infrastructure Live

Agent Service Index (ASI)

The discovery layer for the agent economy.

A secure search endpoint where AI agents can find, self-register, authenticate, purchase, and use services without any human integration step. Services of this shape enable AI agents to reliably use third party services like cloud hosting, domain routing, advertising agencies, and more without fragile and expensive computer-use techniques and without tedious manual setups. These services are only useful if they are discoverable through a secure agent-native search service.

02  /  Consumer Live

Sidequest

For when your friends are busy, but you’re not.

A mobile app for spontaneous local meetups. Broadcast a social activity like a board game or pickleball match to your local area and quickly find people to join you. Or, join someone else's activity. Sidequest solves a critical problem in modern society:

Spontaneous plans are hard. You text twenty friends, fifteen don't reply in time, four are busy, one says 'maybe later.' All the while, there were people in your town wanting to do the same thing. Sidequest helps you find them.
